# Problem Statement ID: 26251

## Problem Statement Title

Indian Army - Predictive Logistics & Forward Supply Chain.

## Description

* Problem Statement: Difficulty in maintaining assured and timely logistics support to forward formations across geographically dispersed and operationally challenging areas. Demand forecasting, inventory levels,transportation capacity, terrain/weather conditions and consumption patterns are maintained across multiple information streams, making it difficult to anticipated requirements and optimise movement of critical supplies.
* Technology Opportunity: AI/ML-based demand forecasting, GIS-enabled logistics planning, loT-based inventory tracking and an integrated predictive logistics management system.
