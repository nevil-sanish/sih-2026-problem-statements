# Problem Statement ID: 26258

## Problem Statement Title

Innovative Mechanized Desludging Models for Varied Climatic Zones and Hilly Terrains

## Description

* Faecal Sludge Management (FSM): FSM entails comprehensive handling of sludge from on-site sanitation systems, ensuring safe collection, transportation, treatment, and disposal. Key challenges include desludging of single pits and septic tanks in hilly and difficult terrains and innovative in-situ management of faecal sludge.
* Background: Conventional vacuum desludging vehicles cannot operate in hilly terrains, narrow lanes, or areas where suction depth requirements exceed equipment capacity. No appropriate mechanized desludging solution exists for these challenging environments.
* Description: Develop low cost innovative mechanized desludging models specifically suited to varied climatic zones and hilly terrains. Address challenges such as: extending reach of equipment beyond current limits, overcoming vacuum suction limitations at depth, and managing viscous sludge that requires excess water or manual stirring.
* Expected Solution: A portable, adaptable low-cost desludging equipment system for varied climate zones & hilly terrains in the specialized features including: (a) reach via flexible hose + booster pump systems for deeper pits, (b) extendable handling of viscous sludge with in-situ dilution or mechanical agitation, (c) easy portability & operable on two-wheelers or handcarts in hilly and narrow-lane settings. Should include safety protocols for operators.
* Potential Impact: Safe FSM in geographically challenging areas; extended reach of mechanized desludging services to all rural households.
